# Output contract

Every checklist design must expose these fields.

## 1. Reliability target

**Purpose:**  
**Avoidable failure(s):**  
**Target user/team:**  

## 2. Operational checklist

For each pause point:

**Pause point / trigger:**  
**Mode:** `READ-DO` or `DO-CONFIRM`  
**Owner/facilitator:**  

### Task checks
Use short observable lines.

### Communication checks
Include only if needed. State who exchanges what information before proceeding.

### Proceed condition
State what permits work to continue when the workflow needs an explicit gate.

## 3. Professional judgment / intentional omissions

State:

- what remains expert judgment;
- important actions intentionally excluded because they do not earn checklist attention;
- domain items that still require subject-matter validation.

## 4. Design rationale

Briefly explain:

- why this pause point;
- why this mode;
- why these are killer items;
- why communication checks are or are not present.

Keep rationale outside the checklist surface.

## 5. Field-test plan

State:

- first test context;
- primary learning question;
- what will be observed;
- revision criterion.

## 6. Outcome measures

Prefer measures tied to the failure pattern:

- missed critical step rate;
- caught near-miss count;
- handoff defect rate;
- delay/rework related to target failure;
- relevant quality/safety outcome;
- teamwork/communication evidence when that is part of the mechanism.

Checkbox completion may be tracked but is not sufficient evidence of effectiveness.
